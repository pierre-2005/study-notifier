People & access

Give each person a separate login. Their courses, tasks, projects, jobs, finance, expenses, schedule, reminders, and phones stay private; you can selectively collaborate by sharing individual Sections.

1Send them the sign-up page

They open /signup on any phone or computer and create their own username and password.

2Approve the account here

Pending accounts cannot sign in until an administrator approves them.

3They connect their phone

After signing in, Settings → Phones & notifications creates their private ntfy topic.

4Share only what you choose

Open a Section and add an approved user as view-only or editable.
