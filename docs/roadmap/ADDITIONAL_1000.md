# MLO: 1,000 additional feature proposals

F1001–F2000 extend the original 1,000-item audit. Reviewed November 2, 2026. These are proposed additions, not shipped capabilities or 1,000 proven omissions. Each group is a candidate subsystem or depth expansion; many share prerequisites. Engineering ideas support coordination around work produced in AutoCAD Electrical, Navisworks and Bluebeam. Native CAD editing, native model rendering, authenticated vendor APIs and engineering calculations are not supplied by this release.

Source links identify inspiration patterns, not claims that every listed proposal exists in the named product. Titles were checked for exact duplicates against the original catalog; related ideas can still share semantics or implementations.
